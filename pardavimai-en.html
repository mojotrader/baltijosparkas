Why it makes sense

An investment that pays off

Šventoji is the fastest-growing seaside resort in Lithuania. Baltijos Parkas is the only homes development in the central part of the village. Your home here is both a residence and a source of rental income.

The neighbourhood is surrounded by well-established infrastructure. Shops, a school, and kindergartens — all within walking distance.

  • Unique location in Šventoji — the only homes development in the centre
  • Modern design and contemporary fixtures
  • Gas underfloor heating and air conditioning in every room
  • Fully furnished home — move in straight away
  • Option to rent short-term or long-term
  • 700 m to the Baltic Sea beach
  • Private gated grounds with landscaping
